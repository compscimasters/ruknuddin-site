Which syllabuses are taught?

Cambridge IGCSE and O Level Computer Science (2210, 0478 and 0984) and Cambridge International AS & A Level Computer Science (9618), covering all papers.

What is the difference between 0478, 0984 and 2210?

They share the same content. 0478 is the IGCSE graded A* to G, 0984 is the IGCSE graded 9 to 1, and 2210 is the O Level. The same classes and notes prepare you for all three.

Which programming language is used?

Lessons use Cambridge-style pseudocode, which you need for the written programming papers, together with Python. For A Level Paper 4 Cambridge allows Python, Java or Visual Basic .NET.

Are the old 9608 and 9691 papers still useful?

Yes, for extra practice. These are earlier A Level syllabuses, and many of their questions cover topics that are still in 9618. Focus on 9618 papers first.
